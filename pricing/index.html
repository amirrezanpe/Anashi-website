Send automated text follow-ups after AI calls. Requires opt-out handling, registration setup, and messaging workflow configuration.

SIP / Phone System Connection
Connect Anashi to your existing clinic phone number or phone system instead of using a new dedicated AI line.

Setup & Onboarding

From signed agreement
to live in 2–4 weeks.

A one-time onboarding fee covers everything needed to configure and launch your AI front desk — AI workflow setup, knowledge base build, phone line configuration, testing, and go-live support.

Setup is quoted with your proposal. The fee varies based on your selected plan, workflows, integrations, and clinic complexity. We'll include it in a clear proposal before you commit to anything.
1
Discovery & Account Setup
We collect clinic info, configure your account, create users, and prepare the knowledge base content.
2
AI Configuration & Testing
We configure your AI workflows, test inbound/outbound flows, and fine-tune based on your feedback.
3
Soft Launch & Refinement
Limited live workflow with real calls. We monitor, gather staff feedback, and resolve any issues.
4
Full Launch & Optimization
Full workflow live. Staff training, performance review, and your dedicated support contact established.
What your clinic provides
Clinic hours, services, providers, insurance info, policies, and FAQs for the knowledge base. Approved responses for common patient questions. Access to your phone system, calendar, or PMS if applicable.
Recommended minimum term
We recommend a 3-month minimum to allow enough time to configure, optimize, and measure results. Most clinics see meaningful workflow improvement within the first month of operation.
BAA & compliance
Business Associate Agreement and HIPAA-related requirements are reviewed during onboarding based on your clinic's specific needs. Call recording and SMS compliance are handled per your preferences.
