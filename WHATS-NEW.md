# Anashi Website — What Changed

## index.html (major upgrade)

New sections, in page order:

1. **Sharpened hero** — outcome-driven headline ("Every call answered. Every appointment confirmed."), medical & dental positioning, upgraded trust bar (added "Live in 2–4 weeks").
2. **The Cost of a Busy Front Desk** (`#problem`) — dark stats band with animated count-up numbers: 30%+ unanswered calls, 1-in-5 no-shows, $200 lost per no-show, 80% of voicemail callers hang up. Labeled as industry averages.
3. **Sample call transcript** (`#listen`) — animated phone mock showing a real call flow, including the mid-call switch to Spanish (your strongest USP made visible). Bubbles animate in on scroll.
4. **ROI Calculator** (`#roi`) — interactive sliders (missed calls/week, no-shows/week, avg appointment value) with live recovered-revenue estimate, payback multiple vs the $399 plan, and a "Get My Custom Proposal" CTA. Conservative assumptions disclosed.
5. **Comparison table** (`#compare`) — Anashi vs full-time hire vs answering service vs voicemail, including monthly cost row.
6. **Integrations** (`#integrations`) — Open Dental, CSV/Excel (any EHR/PMS), calendar, SIP (keep your number), SMS, API.
7. **Founding Clinic Program** (`#founding`) — honest social-proof alternative while you have no public testimonials: direct access to builders, locked founding pricing, roadmap influence.
8. **FAQ accordion** (`#faq`) — 5 top objections answered on the homepage, linking to the full FAQ on the Plans page.

Also: SEO title/description rewritten, Open Graph + Twitter cards, JSON-LD structured data (SoftwareApplication + Organization), nav & footer updated with ROI/Compare links. All CTAs point to https://www.nashboss.com/anashi-fomrs.

## Before you launch — recommended to-dos

- Replace the Founding Clinic section with real testimonials/case studies as soon as you have 2–3 named customers.
- Add a real sample call **audio** file to the `#listen` section when you have a good recording — hearing the AI converts better than reading it.
- Privacy / Terms / HIPAA links in the footer are still plain text — create those pages before running paid traffic.
- Consider a favicon-consistent OG image (1200×630) for social sharing instead of the logo.
