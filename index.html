Everything You Need

Not just a phone bot.
A complete AI front desk.

Every touchpoint in patient communication — managed, automated, and organized in one platform built for medical clinics.

Inbound AI Calls
AI answers every patient call immediately, capturing intent and details — available 24/7, consistent, and always professional.
Outbound Confirmations
AI calls patients to confirm upcoming appointments and updates their status automatically. Reduce no-shows without manual dialing.
Action Required Inbox
Every inbound and outbound call that needs follow-up shows up in one organized inbox — with context, source, and the next step ready to go.
Call Summaries & Recordings
Every call is AI-summarized and stored with a recording. Searchable, indexed, and ready for review or audit at any time.
Multi-language AI
Anashi detects and responds in the patient's language — English, Spanish, French, German, Persian, Arabic, Russian — zero configuration needed.
Reports & Dashboard
Track call volume, confirmation rates, action items, and team performance. Real-time visibility your clinic has never had before.
Appointment Import

Upload your schedule.
AI does the rest.

Import from any CSV or Excel file — straight from your EHR or scheduler. Anashi validates every row, detects duplicates, and schedules AI confirmation calls with your preferred settings.

Smart Row Validation
Anashi checks each row for missing data, format issues, and duplicate patients before scheduling any calls.
Preferred Call Window
Set ideal call times per patient or per batch. Anashi respects time zones and patient preferences automatically.
Patient Notes Before Calling
Annotate individual patients and the AI will naturally incorporate those notes into the confirmation call.
