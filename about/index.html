The Company

Meet NashBoss Inc.

NashBoss Inc is a California-based AI product and automation company building practical software for healthcare operations, business workflows, and customer communication. We started Anashi because we saw the same problem in clinic after clinic: front desks overwhelmed with calls, patients waiting on hold, appointments slipping through the cracks, and staff stretched too thin to keep up.

Anashi is NashBoss Inc's flagship healthcare AI platform, built to help clinics manage patient calls, appointment reminders, follow-ups, and front desk communication. Our team brings experience across software, automation, design, AI workflows, and real-world business operations — and we apply that experience to healthcare communication through Anashi.

Headquartered in California, NashBoss Inc partners with medical clinics, dental practices, and healthcare groups to bring intelligent automation to the patient communication workflow — without replacing the human touch that makes good care possible.

What Drives Us

Principles behind
how we build.

These aren't framed on a wall — they're how we make every product decision at NashBoss Inc.

Reliability Before Hype
A clinic phone line can't just "mostly work." We design for the day-to-day reality of medical practices — where a missed call is a missed patient.
Human Where It Matters
AI handles repetitive work — your team handles the patients who need empathy. Anashi is designed to make your staff more effective, not replace them.
Clarity, Not Complexity
A good dashboard answers questions, not creates new ones. We design for clinic staff — who deserve software that gets out of the way.
Experience & Approach

Software that
actually ships.

Our team brings experience across software, automation, design, AI workflows, and real-world business operations — and we apply that same standard of reliability and craft to every Anashi release.

Built for Production, Not Demos
Every feature in Anashi is designed to operate at clinic scale — handling real calls, real patients, and real workflow constraints, day after day.
Healthcare-Conscious by Design
Patient communication requires thoughtful design, responsible data handling, and strong operational safeguards. Anashi is built to support HIPAA-conscious clinic workflows with controlled access, encryption, configurable retention, healthcare-focused infrastructure, and vendor BAA alignment as part of the implementation process.
A Real Partner, Not a Vendor
We work directly with every clinic that onboards Anashi. You get the team that built the product — not a ticket queue.
